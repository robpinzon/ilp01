# I Love Pampanga (ilovepampanga.com)
> **The Culinary & Heritage Capital of the Philippines**  
> Modern, High-Performance, Mobile-Responsive Informational & Tourism Portal

---

## 🌟 Overview & Identity
**I Love Pampanga** is an informational web portal dedicated to the rich heritage, culinary supremacy, colonial architecture, and natural wonders of the province of Pampanga, Philippines.

- **Website Name:** I Love Pampanga
- **Domain:** `ilovepampanga.com`
- **Visual Aesthetic:** Warm Kapampangan heritage mixed with clean contemporary design:
  - **Warm Terracotta (`#C85A32` / `#9E3C1A`):** Reflecting Spanish-colonial brick heritage, clay kilns, and artisanal cuisine.
  - **Arayat Forest Green (`#1E4D3B` / `#123326`):** Representing the lush rainforest slopes of Mount Arayat and rural riverbanks.
  - **Kapampangan Gold (`#E5A93C` / `#B57F1E`):** Echoing golden lanterns (*Parul Sampernandu*), sunrise over Central Luzon, and turmeric-infused Bringhe.
  - **Warm Parchment Canvas (`#FAF8F5`):** Providing readable contrast and clean typography.
- **Mount Arayat SVG Logo:** Vector emblem featuring the iconic solitary silhouette of Mount Arayat with its signature pinnacle shoulders, rising sun, golden stars, the Rio Grande de Pampanga river curve, and a modern heart motif seamlessly integrated into the typography.

## 🏛️ Key Features & Core Sections

### 1. Sticky Responsive Navbar & Hero Section
- Glassmorphic sticky header with the crisp Mount Arayat SVG vector logo.
- Quick navigation links (Cities & Towns, Culinary Capital, Colonial Churches, Attractions, Language & Quiz).
- Global search trigger with `Ctrl+K` / `⌘K` keyboard shortcut.
- Mobile drawer navigation for small screens.
- Hero banner with tagline, live quick search, jump buttons, and province statistics counter (22 LGUs, 538 official barangays, 1571 founding year, #1 Culinary Capital).

### 2. Cities & Municipalities Directory (PSA & DILG Verified)
- Comprehensive directory covering **all 22 LGUs of Pampanga**:
  - **Angeles City** (Highly Urbanized City) – 33 Official Barangays
  - **City of San Fernando** (Provincial Capital) – 35 Official Barangays
  - **Mabalacat City** (Component City) – 27 Official Barangays
  - **19 Municipalities:** Guagua, Lubao, Arayat, Mexico, Bacolor, Porac, Santa Rita, Magalang, Sasmuan, Candaba, Minalin, Macabebe, Masantol, Floridablanca, Apalit, San Luis, San Simon, Santa Ana, Santo Tomas.
- Filter tabs: *All (22)*, *Cities (3)*, *1st District*, *2nd District*, *3rd District*, *4th District*.
- Real-time search filter to find any town or barangay instantly.
- Each LGU card features:
  - Official classification badge, ZIP code, patron saint, founding year.
  - Historical overview, claim to fame, signature local delicacy.
  - **"Explore Barangays"** interactive modal displaying the verified official PSA barangay directory with real-time in-modal search.

### 3. Culinary Capital: Food Specialty per City/Town
- Traces legendary Kapampangan delicacies to their exact municipal roots:
  - **Angeles City:** Authentic Sizzling Sisig (Lucing "Aling Lucing" Cunanan's heritage).
  - **San Fernando:** Original Kapampangan Tocino (Lolita Hizon / Pampanga's Best) & Tamales Bobotu.
  - **Guagua:** Special Chicharon & authentic Halo-Halo (Razon's / Carreon's).
  - **Santa Rita:** Turrones de Casuy (Ocampo-Lansang since 1920) & seasonal Duman.
  - **Bacolor & Magalang:** Tibok-Tibok (carabao's milk pudding) & Carabao's Milk Pastillas.
  - **Regional Classics:** Bringhe, Crispy Kamaru (mole crickets), Morcon Damulag, Ensaladang Pako (river fern), and Panecillos de San Nicolas.
- Filter pills: *All Flavors*, *Savory*, *Heritage*, *Sweets*, *Exotic*.
- **"View Story & Recipe"** interactive modal highlighting origin history, ingredients, culinary trivia, and verified local restaurants where tourists can taste the dish.

### 4. Historic Churches & Religious Heritage
- Deep architectural profiles of Pampanga’s colonial churches:
  - **San Guillermo Parish Church (Bacolor):** The world-famous "Sunken Church" half-buried by the 1991 Mt. Pinatubo lahar.
  - **Betis Church / St. James the Apostle (Guagua):** The "Sistine Chapel of the Philippines" with monumental ceiling frescoes.
  - **Holy Rosary Parish Church (Santo Rosario, Angeles City):** Minor Basilica of Our Lady of the Most Holy Rosary, with twin-bell towers, 1909 completion, and wartime history.
  - **Metropolitan Cathedral of San Fernando:** Seat of the Archdiocese and historical site of revolutionary history.
  - **St. Augustine Parish Church (Lubao):** Oldest brick church in Pampanga (founded 1572 by Fr. Juan Gallegos).
  - **Santa Monica Parish Church (Minalin):** Unique floral baroque facade and Moorish spires.
  - **St. Peter the Apostle Parish Church (Apalit):** Shrine of *Apung Iru* and the annual Libad fluvial river parade.
- **"Visitor Guide"** modal covering architectural styles, key features, mass schedules, GPS coordinates, and pilgrim etiquette (dress code, photography rules).

### 5. Tourist Spots & Famous Destinations
- **Mount Arayat Protected Landscape:** Hiking trails (Pinnacle Rock, South Peak), freshwater spring pools, and the legend of Mariang Sinukuan (proclaimed a Protected Landscape in April 2022).
- **Giant Lantern Festival (Ligligan Parul):** Asia's Christmas capital showcasing 20-foot light-synchronized kaleidoscopic lanterns.
- **Clark Freeport Zone:** Clark Museum & 4D Theater, Parade Grounds, and former US Air Force base heritage.
- **Aqua Planet:** 10-hectare premier water theme park with 38+ rides.
- **Candaba Swamp & Wild Bird Sanctuary:** Ramsar-level seasonal flyway for over 100,000 migratory waterfowl from Siberia and Japan.
- **SandBox at Alviera (Porac):** Outdoor adventure park with the Philippines' first roller coaster zipline and giant swing.
- **Puning Hot Springs & Volcanic Sand Spa:** Thermal mineral pools and heated volcanic sand therapy deep in the Pinatubo lahar canyon.
- **Pamintuan Mansion:** Museum of Philippine Social History (Santo Entierro St. cor. Miranda St., Angeles City).

### 6. Interactive Kapampangan Phrasebook & Audio Pronunciation
- Features 10 everyday Kapampangan greetings and foodie expressions (*Mayap a abak*, *Manyaman!*, *Mangan tana!*, *Dacal a salamat*).
- Built-in text-to-speech Web Speech API audio playback for authentic pronunciation.

### 7. Interactive Kapampangan Heritage Quiz
- 5-question trivia challenge testing knowledge on food origins, history, and culture.
- Real-time score counter, instant explanations, and achievement badges (*Certified Kapampangan Connoisseur*).

### 8. Suggested 1-Day & 2-Day Travel Itineraries
- **1-Day Classic Heritage & Food Trail:** Angeles Sisig → Guagua Betis Church → Bacolor Sunken Church → San Fernando Giant Lanterns.
- **2-Day Eco-Adventure Trail:** Mount Arayat → Clark Freeport & Aqua Planet → Candaba Bird Sanctuary → Puning Hot Springs.

### 9. Interactive Global Quick Search Modal (`Ctrl+K`)
- Instant live search searching simultaneously across:
  - 22 Cities & Municipalities
  - 538 Verified Barangays (shows which town/city each barangay belongs to)
  - 12 Culinary Dishes
  - 7 Historic Colonial Churches
  - 8 Tourist Destinations

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

- **Markup:** Clean, semantic HTML5 with accessibility attributes (`aria-label`, proper heading hierarchies).
- **Styling:** Modern Tailwind CSS supplemented by `src/styles/main.css` for custom color palettes, glassmorphism, responsive grids, and animations.
- **JavaScript:** Modular Vanilla JavaScript (ES Modules) without heavy dependencies.
- **Data Architecture:** Cleanly organized in `src/data/pampanga-data.js` containing typed objects for LGUs, dishes, churches, spots, phrases, and quiz questions.
- **Animations:** Hardware-accelerated transitions and smooth reveal-on-scroll using `IntersectionObserver`.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

You can preview the website immediately using any local web server:

### Option 1: Python (Built-in)
```bash
# Starts local server on http://localhost:3000
python -m http.server 3000
```
Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your web browser.

### Option 2: Node / NPM
```bash
npm start
```

### Option 3: Vite (Optional Dev Server)
```bash
npx vite
```

## 📚 Official Government & Heritage Data Sources
All geographical data, historical milestones, and cultural details were verified against:
1. **Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA)** – Official Philippine Standard Geographic Code (PSGC) and 2020 Census of Population and Housing.
2. **Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG Region III)** – Official Barangay masterlists and classification tiers.
3. **Department of Tourism (DOT Region III - Central Luzon)** – Official culinary trail guides, ecotourism registrations, and travel advisories.
4. **National Historical Commission of the Philippines (NHCP)** & **National Museum of the Philippines** – Declared National Cultural Treasures (NCT) and National Historical Landmarks.
5. **Provincial Government of Pampanga** – Official municipal histories, provincial festivals, and patronal calendar.
